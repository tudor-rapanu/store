## 1. Dispatch Times

All products are made to order — we don't hold pre-made stock. The estimated dispatch date is shown at checkout before you complete your order, and varies by product. During busy periods (e.g. holiday season), production and dispatch may take longer than usual; we'll do our best to keep any delays to a minimum.

## 2. Delivery — Romania

Domestic orders are delivered by **Sameday**, with two options at checkout:

- **Easybox / locker pickup** — 15 RON
- **Home address delivery** — 25 RON

**Free shipping on domestic orders over 150 RON.** Once dispatched, Sameday delivery typically takes **1–3 business days**, with possible delays during busy periods (holidays, sales events).

## 3. Delivery — International

We now ship internationally via **Poșta Română**, at a flat rate of 75 RON. Delivery times vary by destination country and are longer than domestic delivery; an estimate is shown at checkout where available.

## 4. Returns & Refunds

You have **14 days** from receiving your order to return it. If you're not happy with your item, just get in touch at [contact@toddsthreads.com](mailto:contact@toddsthreads.com) and we'll either **replace it or issue a refund** — whichever you prefer. Refunds are returned using your original payment method (bank transfer or back to your card via Netopia).

## 5. Questions

For anything about your order, delivery, or a return, contact us at [contact@toddsthreads.com](mailto:contact@toddsthreads.com).
