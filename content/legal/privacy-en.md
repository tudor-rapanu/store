## 2. What We Collect and Why

We collect personal data you provide directly (name, email, phone, shipping/billing address) and data generated automatically as you use the site (order history, approximate location from IP, on-site behavior via analytics, once you've given consent). We use it to:

- Process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and customer support
- Manage your account, if you create one
- Send order-related communications (confirmations, shipping updates, download links for digital products)
- Analyze and improve the site and our product offering
- Send marketing communications, only if you've opted in — you can withdraw consent at any time
- Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

## 3. Where Your Data Is Processed

Your data is processed and stored within the European Union, across the following service providers, each acting as our data processor under a data processing agreement:

- **Google Cloud** — application hosting (Cloud Run, EU region)
- **Neon** — database hosting (EU region)
- **Cloudflare** — content delivery, security, and image hosting
- **Netopia Payments** — card payment processing; we never see or store your full card details
- **Zoho** — business email
- **Amazon SES / Resend** — transactional order emails
- **Sameday** and **Poșta Română** — courier delivery; we share your name, address, and phone number only as needed to deliver your order
- **PostHog** (EU Cloud) — product analytics, only after you accept cookies via our consent banner

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with third parties where necessary to provide the service described above, to comply with a legal obligation, or to protect our rights (for example, in cases of suspected fraud).

## 4. Cookies

We use a cookie consent banner when you first visit the site. Until you accept, only strictly necessary cookies (required for the site to function, e.g. your cart and language preference) are set. Once you accept, we also load PostHog analytics cookies to understand how the site is used and improve it. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site or adjusting your browser's cookie settings, which will stop analytics cookies from being set on future visits.

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as necessary to provide our services, plus any additional period required by law — for example, Romanian accounting and tax law requires certain order and invoice records to be retained for a statutory period. Marketing data is kept until you withdraw consent.

## 6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, or to close your account, email us at [contact@toddsthreads.com](mailto:contact@toddsthreads.com).

If you believe we haven't handled your data properly, you can also file a complaint with Romania's data protection authority, ANSPDCP ([dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro)).
